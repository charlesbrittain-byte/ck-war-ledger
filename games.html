The button makes a read-only key covering the usual Torn stats — battle stats, personal stats, perks, bars, job, crimes and so on — so new features can be added later without asking you for a new key. It deliberately leaves out the private things a full Limited key would include: it cannot read your mail, your events, your trades or your logs. You make it yourself, it is stored only in this browser, and it is only ever sent to api.torn.com — nobody here can read your battle stats but you, which is why you record your own.

The clubhouse records for you — connecting your key is all it takes. The tracker reads your stats for you: once when an event opens, regularly through it, and once at the end. No tapping, and your starting point is caught even if you are asleep. Your key is only ever used to read your own battle stats, and only percentages are ever shared — never your actual stats. Worth knowing: the board shows Raw and Adjusted side by side, and comparing the two gives a rough idea of how big someone's stats are. It is a friendly faction competition, so that is the point, but it is your call.

Adjusted % for this event uses an estimated starting split: per-stat recording only began part way through, so your four stats as they are now have been wound back to the total you started on. It is exact for anyone under the cap, and close for everyone else. Events from here on record the real split from the first second.

Ranking is on Adjusted %. Smaller players gain a bigger percentage for the same work, and above 50M per stat Torn heavily dampens gains, so raw % gain is a poor measure at both ends. Adjusted does two things: it caps each of your four stats at 50M, and it tilts the whole scale so that being big is less of a penalty. It is a fixed calculation — your score does not depend on who else entered. Smaller players are still favoured, just not by the length of the street.

A ⚡ multiplier beside a name means that person gained several times more than the energy they got through can normally buy — which is what a happy jump looks like from the outside. It is shown for interest and does not change anyone's score; jumps are allowed. Nothing appears for ordinary training.

Fac xan is what you have taken from the faction armoury, counted from the moment the event opened. Own xan is xanax you took that did not come from the faction, so the two together are everything you took. Refills and Cans come from your own stats too. None of them affect the score — they are there so you can see who put the effort in.

There is nothing to press. Connect your key once — before the event if you like — and the tracker takes your starting point the moment it opens, reads you every few minutes through it, and takes a final reading at the end. Join late and only the rest of the event counts for you, which the board shows beside your name: Torn keeps no history of battle stats, so nothing can be counted from before your first reading.

For this event Refills and Cans started counting part way through, when they were added. Xanax is complete, because the faction armoury log goes back. Future events count everything from the start.
