Who's joining — and who isn't

MemberJoinedDays in / idleLast joinedIn one nowUsual role

Everyone on the roster, slackers first. "In one now" counts crimes still recruiting or planning, so someone who has joined the next one is not chased for the last one. Members who joined the faction inside the window are marked with *, and their days in / idle are counted only from the day they arrived — judging them on crimes that ran before they were here would be meaningless. The percentage beside a current crime is their pass rate in that slot — green at 65 and over, amber to 55, red below — so a bad fit can be swapped before it runs.

Hit quality — who's punching down

MemberWar hitsAvg respect / hitAvg fair fightAvg target lvlLow-value hits

Fair fight runs from 1.00 (far below you — pays almost nothing) to 3.00 (an even or brave fight). A low-value hit here is fair fight ≤ 1.25: fine occasionally, but members living in this column are farming easy targets that would serve your newer players better. Sorted worst-first.
